## Bound question

After terminal T2 P3, search for exactly one materially new, zero-cost and
lawfully retainable source class for XAUUSD or major-FX M5/M15. A candidate had
to provide all of the following before any price outcome, hypothesis, code or
backtest could open:

1. reconstructable point-in-time history from at least 2018;
2. a matching live/update contract;
3. a field outside price, spread, tick volume, calendar timing, generic
   sentiment and the frozen failure catalog;
4. a predeclared signed effect for the first or second closed M5/M15 bar after
   realistic retail cost; and
5. a natural independent cadence of 2-5 events/week with balanced directions,
   without selecting a tail or threshold from outcomes.
