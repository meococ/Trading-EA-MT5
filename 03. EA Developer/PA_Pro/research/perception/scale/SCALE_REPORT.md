the way (FINDINGS F2): `asia_convert` mutates RANGE_OPEN→BOX in place,

days of warmup (keeps runs fast; undercounts only objects born >5 days
earlier — noted as a limitation).

## Q3 — other pairs (30 random days each, both arms)

GBPUSD and AUDUSD sit on the EURUSD template exactly (same pip size
1e-4): struct med 3–4, over5 ≈ 0, asia emU ~9–13/100, same birth
pattern. AUDUSD runs marginally sparser (asia BRACKET 1.3 vs ~1.7).

**USDJPY is the outlier — and it is a pip-scale bug, not a session
regime.** `engine.PIP = 1e4` is hardcoded (FINDINGS F3): JPY's true pip
is 1e-2, so every pip-denominated threshold behaves 100× loose and the
20/50 round grid never lands on real JPY rounds (110.00/110.50 are 5 000
engine units apart). Symptoms in the table: boxH_abr med **12–15**
(author 3.45, EURUSD ~5), box_dur ~100–150 bars, `rn50w2` meaningless in
engine units. GBPUSD/AUDUSD grids are correct (00/50 = 50 engine pips).

## What the build lane should look at

1. **Clutter is a scale-wide fact, not a TUNE artifact** — 3–4.5× the
   author's live-object count on every year/session/pair/TF, while the
   >5 hard budget is essentially never breached. The engine spreads the
   budget; the author clusters into one or two objects.
2. **Re-anchor rate** — ~4–6 logged edge moves/100 bars on every cell;
   the author's ink does not move. `lines._eval` re-anchoring dominates;
   consider a higher re-anchor bar or hysteresis.
3. **Births mix vs author** — over-supply of CONTEXT_LINE (up to 32×),
   LEVEL_CARRIED (~2–10×), BRACKET and asia BOX; under-supply of
   MINI_LEVEL (~⅓). Consistent with R37.4 (levels are a proposal-
   coverage problem, not a rate problem).
4. **Line slope population** — engine lines run ~1.6–3.9 p/hr median vs
   the author's 9.2; the drawn lines are 3–4× too flat at scale.
5. **Throughput wall (FINDINGS F1)** — `swings.alive`/`_floors` +
   `engine._abr` inner calls make multi-week runs quadratic; fix before
   any continuous-run ambition.
6. **STAND_ASIDE fires on ~55–70% of bars** — mostly `chop`. Check
   whether the ER window/threshold is calibrated to M5 DESIGN bars.

## What the BOX lane should look at

1. **asia RANGE_OPEN forming moves are silent** (FINDINGS F4) —
   `boxes.py:713` rewrites top/bottom/t1 every bar with no event.
   Decide: log a `track` event, or exempt forming ranges in the spec.
2. **asia box over-birth** ~3× author (and box_dur ~2×+ — partly the
   converted range carrying its forming span as duration).
3. **`render.py` object layer divides by PIP where it must multiply**
   (FINDINGS F5) — QA overlays drawing engine objects at price scale
   are silently off-canvas; verify no evalcheck image relied on it.

## Limitations (stated, not hidden)

- Day-mode + 5-trading-day warmup: objects born earlier (long
  CONTEXT_LINEs, old LEVEL_CARRIED) are undercounted — biases clutter
  *down*, so the 3–4× density gap is if anything understated.
- `struct med` = median of per-(day,session) medians; >5/>8 shares are
  bar-weighted pooled. Family medians in TABLES.md are per-session.
- Engine `abr_len` is 20 bars; spec tolerance language speaks of a
  50-bar ABR — the author row uses the cached causal abr50. Close, not
  identical; documented rather than equated.
- asia `box_dur` includes the RANGE_OPEN forming span (t_left = range
  start) — inflates asia duration vs the author's drawn-rectangle span.
- Sunday-open days (~8 bars) stay in the sample; their rows are noise.
- JPY row statistics are measured in engine units — true-pip conversion
  applied only to `rn50w2`; the table is diagnostic, not comparable.
