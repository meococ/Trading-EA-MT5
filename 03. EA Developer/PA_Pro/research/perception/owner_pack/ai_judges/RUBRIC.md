# How a Volman-style price-action trader marks a EUR/USD M5 chart (rubric for blind tasks)

Context: 5-minute EUR/USD candles, one trading day at a time. Grey line = EMA25. Faint dashed horizontals = round numbers (00/50), which are platform grid only - never drawn as structure. The vertical dashed line = "now" (the decision moment).

The trader keeps the chart almost bare: typically 1-3 objects, rarely more than 5. Every object must be the operative edge of the CURRENT structure near price. He draws three kinds of object:

BOX (rectangle) - a congestion block: several bars overlapping sideways (a pause, a flag, a range, a pullback base).
- Top and bottom edges sit on CLUSTERS of wicks (at least 2 touches within 1-2 pips), not on a single isolated spike; a lone wick poking out is tolerated (it is a tease), the edge stays at the cluster.
- The left side starts where the congestion starts; the right side reaches the last bar of the block (or "now" if price is still inside).
- Usually the most recent block; typical height 6-34 pips; it contains most of its bars' bodies.

SLOPED LINE - the edge of a pattern (flag edge, pullback line, trend line under higher lows / over lower highs, wedge side).
- Passes through at least 2 swing extremes (wick tips) on its defended side, within about 1-2 pips; a third contact confirms it.
- It is an edge, not a line through the middle of price: bars sit on one side; pokes that close back are teases.
- Slope rule: a line above a congestion should not rise steeply through it (that is a chord, not resistance); likewise below.
- It belongs to the newest structure near price, not an old leg far away.

HORIZONTAL LEVEL - a remembered price where the market turned or paused before: an old block edge, a double top/bottom, a dome or ceiling that was tested, a session extreme.
- It matters only if price is near it now (it is an obstacle, magnet or trigger for the next move).
- Round numbers alone are not levels.

NOT drawn: objects inside unreadable chop; objects far from price or long stale; fits through candle bodies; channels; Fibonacci; stop/target lines.

Answer at the dashed line, as if the bars to its right do not exist yet.
