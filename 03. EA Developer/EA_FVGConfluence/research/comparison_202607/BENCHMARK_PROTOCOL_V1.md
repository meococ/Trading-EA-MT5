## Public cohort freeze procedure

Pass 1 discovers the public landscape without return ranking. Inclusion covers
full-auto MT5/FX products whose primary mechanism contains three-candle FVG,
liquidity sweep, MSS/BOS, OB, retest, or session state. Indicator-only,
manual-assistant, grid, martingale, and copied products remain in the ledger
with an exclusion reason.

Pass 2 begins only after the URL/entity cohort is hash-frozen. Performance
grades are:

- `A`: independent live history with full account verification.
- `B`: source/demo reproducible on hash-bound data.
- `C`: vendor copy, screenshot, review, or vendor backtest.

Grade C supports architecture comparison only. Performance comparison requires
at least five grade A/B accounts, each with at least 36 months, 200 closed
trades, no hidden or custom-start history, and observable deposits/withdrawals.
Otherwise the verdict is `INSUFFICIENT_VERIFIED_DATA`.
